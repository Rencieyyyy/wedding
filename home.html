Our Story

It started with a single notification. In a world where millions scroll past each other every day, Myca decided not to wait. Breaking the unwritten rules of online dating, she took the leap and sent the very first message to Harold. It was simple, brave, and the exact moment their universe changed forever.

What began as a digital hello quickly transformed into late-night video calls, unforgettable first dates, and a deep, undeniable love. Three beautiful years of shared laughter, growth, and endless support have flown by, proving that a single click can lead to a lifetime.

Now, they are ready to delete the app for good and log into forever.
